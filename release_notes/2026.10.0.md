First release: Home Assistant learns which quick actions you click at this time of day and on this day of the week, and ranks them in one sensor your dashboard can read.

### ⭐ Suggestions

Add an action per quick action — a name and the entities whose clicks count for it — and `sensor.<name>_ranking` lists, in its `top` attribute, the ones you usually start now. Only clicks made by a person count: automations, physical buttons and voice satellites never do, and a script that switches something else on counts once, for the script. A habit needs about two uses at the same moment to show up, and fades with a 14-day half-life once you stop.

### 🔍 Diagnostics

The `ranking` attribute shows every action's score, the debug log says why each click was or was not counted, and the diagnostics download holds the full weekday × hour grids.
